# Risk Management – Threat, Vulnerability & Compliance Investigations

## Overview

This course focused on identifying, analyzing, and mitigating risk within enterprise systems using structured frameworks and real-world tools. Through hands-on investigations, I assessed vulnerabilities, conducted risk analysis, built asset inventories, and developed mitigation strategies aligned with industry standards such as NIST SP 800-30 and PCI DSS.

---

## Security Investigations

### Vulnerability Exploitation Investigation: Identifying & Exploiting System Weaknesses

* **Objective:** Identify system vulnerabilities and demonstrate exploitation techniques

* **Tools:** Metasploit, NVD, Rapid7

* **Skills:** Vulnerability research, exploitation, CVE analysis

* **Key Findings:**

  * Identified critical SMB vulnerability (CVE-2017-0143) and analyzed exploit details 
  * Used Metasploit to gain system-level access to a Windows server
  * Retrieved sensitive data, demonstrating impact of unpatched vulnerabilities

---

### Risk Assessment Investigation: NIST SP 800-30 Threat & Impact Analysis

* **Objective:** Conduct a structured risk assessment using NIST methodology

* **Skills:** Threat modeling, likelihood analysis, impact evaluation

* **Key Findings:**

  * Identified adversarial outsider as primary threat source
  * Determined likelihood and impact as “likely” and “high” respectively 
  * Evaluated risks across confidentiality, integrity, and availability

---

### Compliance Investigation: PCI DSS Risk & Control Review

* **Objective:** Evaluate system compliance with PCI DSS requirements

* **Tools:** SimpleRisk

* **Skills:** Compliance analysis, risk tracking, control validation

* **Key Findings:**

  * Identified multiple instances of noncompliance within risk tracking system 
  * Created and reviewed risk entries and mitigation actions
  * Analyzed inherent vs residual risk within compliance framework

---

### Asset Discovery Investigation: Network Scanning & Asset Inventory

* **Objective:** Identify and document assets within a network environment

* **Tools:** Nmap, Nbtscan, SimpleRisk

* **Skills:** Asset discovery, OS fingerprinting, inventory management

* **Key Findings:**

  * Discovered hosts and services across network using scanning tools 
  * Identified operating systems and mapped asset details
  * Documented assets within a centralized risk management system

---

### Vulnerability Management Investigation: Scanning & Risk Documentation

* **Objective:** Identify and document technical vulnerabilities across systems

* **Tools:** Nmap, Greenbone (GVM), SimpleRisk

* **Skills:** Vulnerability scanning, risk documentation, exposure analysis

* **Key Findings:**

  * Detected misconfigurations such as anonymous FTP access 
  * Identified SMB vulnerabilities and open ports across hosts
  * Documented vulnerabilities and associated risks within SimpleRisk

---

### Risk Mitigation Investigation: Control Implementation & Risk Reduction

* **Objective:** Develop and evaluate risk mitigation strategies

* **Tools:** SimpleRisk

* **Skills:** Risk reduction, control implementation, mitigation planning

* **Key Findings:**

  * Reduced inherent risk through implementation of security controls 
  * Evaluated residual risk after mitigation strategies were applied
  * Proposed controls such as encryption, data classification, and monitoring

---
