# Data Privacy (MCY 620)

## Overview

This course focused on privacy engineering, regulatory frameworks, and practical methods for protecting sensitive data. The work emphasizes Privacy by Design principles, risk assessment methodologies, and the use of Privacy Enhancing Technologies (PETs) to mitigate real-world privacy threats.

---

## Assignments

### Privacy Enhancing Technologies (PETs) Hands-On Analysis

* **Objective:** Evaluate real-world PET tools and analyze how they mitigate privacy risks

* **Skills:** Privacy analysis, anonymization techniques, encryption concepts, tool evaluation

* **Key Takeaways:**

  * Identified risks in visual data exposure and facial recognition systems
  * Evaluated anonymization and encryption techniques in simulated environments
  * Analyzed tools such as Tor Browser, Anonymous Camera, and privacy scoring extensions

* [View Assignment](pet-handout/PET-Hands-On-Work.pdf) 

---

### Privacy Risk Assessment & Privacy by Design (Final Project)

* **Objective:** Analyze how Privacy Risk Assessments (PRA) support secure system design

* **Skills:** Risk assessment, data flow analysis, privacy engineering, regulatory alignment

* **Key Takeaways:**

  * Explored how organizations identify and mitigate privacy risks early in system design 
  * Connected PRA to frameworks such as NIST and GDPR
  * Examined real-world risks including data overcollection, unauthorized access, and third-party exposure
  * Demonstrated how Privacy by Design improves system security, compliance, and user trust

* [View Final Project](final-project/Privacy-Risk-Assessment.pdf)

## Key Skills Developed

* Privacy Risk Assessment (PRA)
* Privacy by Design principles
* Data protection and regulatory awareness (GDPR, NIST frameworks)
* Anonymization and encryption techniques
* Threat identification in data processing systems

## Summary

This coursework demonstrates the ability to analyze privacy risks, evaluate protective technologies, and apply structured methodologies to secure sensitive data. The projects reflect both theoretical understanding and practical application of modern privacy engineering concepts.
